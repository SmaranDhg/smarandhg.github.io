Sheet 02
About
The human side

I'm an engineer from Kathmandu who's spent the last couple of years moving through problems that don't share a tech stack on purpose. I've built a columnar data warehouse, written spec-matching software for mechanical parts, and now help design Zero Trust identity systems for AI agents — including contributing to SAFE-MCP, an open-source safety framework under the Linux Foundation.

What carries across all of it is the same habit: walk into a domain I haven't worked in, figure out how it actually behaves, and ship something that holds up. I've done that as a Data Engineer, a Lead Backend Engineer, and now a Senior AI Engineer — in that order, in about two years.

Off the clock: I co-founded an ed-tech startup in college because we couldn't find decent study resources ourselves, served as software coordinator for my campus NGO, and once wrote and directed a play that was, by campus consensus, the hit of the event.

— S. Dhungana
