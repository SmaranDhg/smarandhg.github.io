I am a computer engineer and AI researcher from Kathmandu, Nepal, pursuing graduate Ph.D. research in Computer Science & Engineering. My work explores the critical intersection of AI safety, Zero Trust architectures, and distributed systems — asking how we can formally bound, verify, and coordinate autonomous agents that perceive, decide, and actuate with minimal human intervention.
